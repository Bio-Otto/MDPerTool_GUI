# Literature-Validated 3-Structure Panel for MDPerTool

This panel was selected to provide diverse, literature-backed allosteric systems.

## Structures

1) 1V4S - Human glucokinase (allosteric enzyme)
- RCSB title: Crystal structure of human glucokinase
- Primary citation: Structure (2004) 12:429-438
- DOI: 10.1016/j.str.2004.02.005
- Why included: classic monomeric allostery with reported active/inactive forms and allosteric site.

2) 1RAA - E. coli aspartate transcarbamoylase (ATCase), CTP-ligated T-state
- RCSB title includes implications for ATCase mutants and negative cooperativity
- Primary citation: Proteins (1993) 15:147-176
- DOI: 10.1002/prot.340150206
- Why included: canonical allosteric system with structure-linked mutagenesis interpretation.

3) 2HHB - Human deoxyhemoglobin
- Primary citation: J Mol Biol (1984) 175:159-174
- DOI: 10.1016/0022-2836(84)90472-8
- Why included: historically validated cooperative allostery in a multimeric assembly.

## Suggested first perturbation seeds

- 1V4S: residues near the allosteric activator pocket and glucose-binding region.
- 1RAA: regulatory-chain residues at the CTP interface first, then catalytic-regulatory interfaces.
- 2HHB: alpha1-beta2 interface and heme-proximal neighborhoods for pathway contrast.
